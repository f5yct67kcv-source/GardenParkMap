Grantown-on-Spey – Your Perfect Highland Base

Perfectly positioned to explore Strathspey & Badenoch, Speyside, Morayshire, Nairnshire, Inverness and the Moray Firth coast, Grantown-on-Spey is the ideal north east Highland base. This interactive map highlights 50+ carefully curated destinations, from world-renowned whisky distilleries and historic castles to scenic beaches, wildlife hotspots, family activities, and excellent local restaurants. Each location includes useful links, postcodes, and contact details, making it easy for you to plan your day trips or simply discover the region at your own pace. Click on a category to explore whisky experiences, town highlights, or ready-made day trips – your Highland adventure starts here! With around 40 hotels, B&Bs, guest houses, camping and self-catering options, as well as a wide range of shops, cafés and restaurants, Grantown-on-Spey is not only a gateway to multiple Highland regions, but also an excellent place to base yourself for a few days while exploring them in star shaped day trips.

🗺️ Day Trip Ideas

Curated itineraries for your Highland adventure – Click to expand

🐬 Dolphin & Whisky Day – 6-8 hours

Watch dolphins at Chanonry Point, visit Glenmorangie distillery, and if time allows, take the Nigg Ferry.

Route:

  • 🐬 Chanonry Point – Dolphin watching (best 1.5-2h after low tide)
  • ⚗️ Glenmorangie – Famous Highland distillery tour
  • ⛴️ Nigg Ferry – Historic 2-car ferry (if time allows)
  • 🏰 Optional: Dunrobin Castle – fairytale castle
  • 🍽️ Eat Out: Cawdor Tavern or Uilebheist Distillery & Brewery

⚠️ Note: Please check opening hours, tour times, and availability before visiting. Phone numbers and websites are provided for direct planning.

🏰 Castle & Whisky – ~6 hours

Explore the Pearl of the North castle and visit authentic Speyside distilleries.

Route:

  • 🏰 Ballindalloch Castle – Pearl of the North with Highland cattle
  • ⚗️ Speyburn – Daniel's recommended distillery tour
  • ⚗️ Speyside Cooperage – Watch master coopers craft barrels
  • 🍽️ Eat Out: Dinner in Grantown-on-Spey

⚠️ Note: Please check opening hours, tour times, and availability before visiting. Phone numbers and websites are provided for direct planning.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Fun Day – ~6 hours

Perfect family adventure with forest trails, reindeer, and Highland history.

Route:

  • 🌲 Landmark Forest Adventure – Treetop trails & waterslides
  • 🦌 Cairngorm Reindeer – Meet Britain's only reindeer herd
  • 🏚️ Highland Folk Museum – Open-air museum with 35+ historic buildings. Highland life 1700s-1950s.
  • 🚡 Optional: Cairngorm Funicular – mountain railway
  • 🍽️ Eat Out: Pack a picnic or café at Landmark

⚠️ Note: Please check opening hours, tour times, and availability before visiting. Phone numbers and websites are provided for direct planning.

🏖️ Coastal Adventure – 6.5-8 hours

Explore the stunning Moray coast with seals, beaches, and charming villages.

Route:

  • 🏖️ Cullen – Stunning beach & Bow Fiddle Rock
  • 🦭 Portgordon – Grey seal watching (best at high tide)
  • ⛵ Findhorn – Picturesque village & eco-community
  • 🍽️ Eat Out: Crown & Anchor Inn or The Admirals

⚠️ Note: Please check opening hours, tour times, and availability before visiting. Phone numbers and websites are provided for direct planning.

🦕 Loch Ness Explorer – 5.5-7 hours

The classic Loch Ness experience with waterfalls, canal locks, and maybe Nessie!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Grantown-on-Spey such a good base for exploring the Highlands?
Grantown sits in the centre of multiple regions. Guests can easily make star-shaped day trips into Speyside, the Cairngorms, Moray Coast, Inverness area, Strathspey and Aberdeenshire. Many highlights are reachable within 20–50 minutes. Every day can offer completely different scenery and experiences.
How far is Inverness from Grantown-on-Spey?
Approximately 52 minutes by car. You can reach Inverness either via Nairn or via the A9. Public transport is also available — regular buses connect Grantown-on-Spey and Inverness.
What shouldn't you miss in the region?
If time is limited, we recommend combining Inverness, Loch Ness and Speyside. This gives you the historic Highland capital, the famous Loch Ness landscape and the world-famous whisky region of Speyside — a perfect mix of culture, nature and history.
Is Nairn worth a visit?
Absolutely. Nairn has one of the finest sandy beaches in the region, a pleasant promenade and several cosy cafés. On a clear day the views across the Moray Firth are spectacular, and with a little luck you might even spot dolphins from the shore.
Where is the best whisky in Scotland?
Who knows? That wasn't very funny, was it? 😉 The truth is nobody can answer that question. Scotland has more than 140 distilleries and everyone has different tastes. You may love a smoky island whisky or perhaps you are completely new to whisky. If so, Speyside is one of the best places to begin. Why not stay with us and explore more than 50 distilleries throughout Speyside and Strathspey? A wonderful adventure for whisky lovers and future whisky lovers alike.

Yes, really — and they have never charged an entry fee! The best location is Chanonry Point near Fortrose, where Bottlenose Dolphins are regularly spotted, especially 1.5–2 hours after low tide. Bring binoculars and be patient — the reward is worth it.
Where can I find the official government website to apply for a UK ETA?
To avoid third-party scam sites or unnecessary extra processing fees, you should only apply through official UK government channels. You have two options: apply online directly on the official UK government website at gov.uk/eta/apply, or use the official 'UK ETA' app (Apple App Store and Google Play Store), which lets you easily scan your passport's biometric chip and your face. Important: the official UK ETA application costs £20. Always check that the website address ends exactly in .gov.uk — independent websites often look identical to official government pages but charge large hidden fees on top of the standard rate.
